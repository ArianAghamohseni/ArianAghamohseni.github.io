Sharif University of Technology — RIML Lab

DiffPM — Diffusion-based time-series modeling

Diffusion-based generation of multivariate time series, separating low-frequency trends from high-frequency residuals and combining window-level samples into coherent sequences. This work explores generative modeling for time-series synthesis, representation learning, and downstream forecasting.

Supervisors: Dr. Mohammad Hossein Rohban, Dr. Babak Khalaj

Explore DiffPM ↗
Bandit researcher
Sharif University of Technology

Non-stationary bandits

I work on regret analysis for non-stationary linear bandits, studying how changing environments affect exploration, elimination, and sequential decision-making. The focus is on understanding the theoretical behavior of learning algorithms under distributional drift.

Supervisors: Dr. Yasin Abbasi, Prof. András György

Multimodal research
Vision-language models

VLM reasoning

Research into reasoning in vision-language models: how visual evidence informs multi-step reasoning, and how to evaluate the resulting behavior. I’m interested in the connection between visual understanding and the reasoning processes of multimodal models.
