Sharif University of Technology — RIML Lab

Text-to-Image Generative Models

We study inference-time and prompt/embedding optimization for text-to-image diffusion models. Our current focus includes defining losses aligned with conditioned Stable Diffusion objectives and a reward-driven prompt-embedding optimization procedure that couples principled reward selection with exploration to improve visual/textual alignment.

Supervisors: Dr. Mohammad Hossein Rohban, Dr. Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah

Started September 2025
Sharif University of Technology — MLL Lab

Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning in LLMs

We develop an RL-based optimization layer that adapts a model's reasoning policy during inference, trading off exploration depth and decisive steps via a shaped reward. The layer tunes sampling temperature, halting criteria, and verifier thresholds to minimize expected reasoning cost at a target accuracy. Early results improve pass@k under a fixed budget and reduce compute.

Supervisor: Dr. Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah

Started May 2025
Sharif University of Technology

Non-Stationary Bandit Regret Analysis

We analyze non-stationary linear bandits using geometric intuition and a four-way partition (combinatorial vs. continuous; full-space vs. restricted) to bound the first stage of the META algorithm. We derive new elimination criteria and show that, under mild drift assumptions, the algorithm attains regret rates comparable to the stationary case.

Supervisors: Dr. Yasin Abbasi, Prof. András György

Nov 2024–Oct 2025
Sharif University of Technology — RIML Lab

Multivariate Probabilistic Time-Series Representation Learning (DiffPM)

We developed DiffPM, a diffusion-based framework that factorizes multivariate sequences into low-frequency trends and high-frequency residuals, trains window-conditioned denoisers with position-aware stitching, and yields calibrated probabilistic embeddings supporting whole-series synthesis, reconstruction, and downstream forecasting with strong temporal coherence and efficient sampling.

Supervisors: Dr. Mohammad Hossein Rohban, Dr. Babak Khalaj

Apr 2025–Oct 2025
Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)

Representation Learning with Diffusion Models

We use conditioned diffusion models for transferable representation learning: we integrate classifier-free guidance into the conditioned process and shape the noise schedule/variance to extract disentangled feature maps, which are then transferred to a fine-tuned ResNet for image classification.

Supervisor: Dr. Ahmad Nickabadi

Sep 2024–Mar 2025
Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)

Generative Models (Diffusion & DiTs)

We improved sample quality and efficiency for diffusion models and Diffusion Transformers by combining token merging and caching with VAE-inspired latent regularization, achieving better FID alongside smoother latent space trajectories and lower compute.

Supervisor: Dr. Ahmad Nickabadi
